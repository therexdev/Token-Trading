Where the losses go on other DEXs

What "lossless" actually means.

No fee, on either side

Makers and takers both trade at 0%. There's no protocol fee baked into the price and no hidden spread — the orderbook matches you at the resting order's price, exactly.

No gas, ever

Koinos replaced gas with mana — a resource that recharges on its own. With the network's free mana sharer, your orders cost nothing to submit even if you hold no KOIN.

No slippage, no MEV

An AMM prices you along a bonding curve, so size moves the price against you and bots front-run the difference. An orderbook just fills your limit at the price you chose.

No custody risk

Funds are locked in the contract only while your order rests, and released the instant it fills or you cancel. The contract has no withdrawal or admin function — no one can touch your balance.
